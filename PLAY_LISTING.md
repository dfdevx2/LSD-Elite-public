# Google Play listing (English, en-US)

**App name (max 30):** LSD Elite: Local AI

**Short description (max 80):**
Private AI chat and coding that runs offline on your Snapdragon phone.

**Full description (max 4000):**
LSD Elite runs powerful open AI models directly on your Snapdragon phone or handheld. No account, no subscription, no cloud: after the first model download everything works offline and your conversations never leave your device.

WHY LSD ELITE
• 100% on-device — uses the Snapdragon GPU and NPU, never the cloud
• Chat mode and Code mode, with separate history for every conversation
• Projects: group chats in folders and give each folder its own instructions
• Switch between several downloaded models from the top of the chat
• "Think more" switch for step-by-step reasoning (Qwen3)
• Live voice mode using Android's on-device speech recognition and text-to-speech
• Replies keep generating in the background and notify you when they're done
• Material You colors and an optional pure-black AMOLED theme
• Available in English, Português, Deutsch, Español, Français, हिन्दी, Italiano, 日本語, Русский and 中文

MODELS
Download only what you want: Qwen3 8B and 4B, Qwen 2.5 7B Instruct and Qwen 2.5 Coder 7B, all under the Apache-2.0 license. Models are large (about 2.5 GB to 5 GB), so use Wi-Fi for the first download.

PRIVACY
No accounts, no analytics, no ads. The app uses the internet only to download models.

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 12 or newer
• A Snapdragon device (there is no CPU fallback: other chips are not supported)
• 12 GB RAM or more recommended for the 8B models; 8 GB for the 4B
