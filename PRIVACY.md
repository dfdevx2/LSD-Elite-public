# Privacy Policy — LSD Elite

_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

LSD Elite ("the app") is developed by DFDX047 ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## Summary

The app does **not** collect, store on servers, or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no ads.

## Data that stays on your device

- Conversations, folders, instructions and settings are stored locally in the app's private storage.
- Text and voice you send to a model are processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded for processing.
- Voice input uses Android's on-device speech recognition and text-to-speech provided by your system. The app does not receive or store audio.
- Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

## Network use

The app connects to the internet only to:
- download AI model files (from the hosting services listed in the model picker, such as Hugging Face), and
- fetch the list of available models.

Those services may see your IP address and standard request information, as with any download. I do not receive that information.

## Permissions

- **Internet / network state** — to download models.
- **Notifications** — to tell you when a reply finishes while the app is in the background.
- **Foreground service** — to keep generating a reply while the app is in the background.
- **Microphone** — only when you use voice input.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
